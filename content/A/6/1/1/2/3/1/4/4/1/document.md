###### A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.4.1 - Delegate Onboarding Process [Core]

The Grove Foundation manages Delegate onboarding. The Grove Foundation may onboard a Delegate at its discretion. The Grove Foundation conducts identity verification, conflict-of-interest collection, and sanctions and undue-risk checks as it deems necessary. Upon acceptance, the Foundation notifies the Operational Facilitator, who updates [A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.8 - Registry of Delegates](ce2e0c1b-be0f-468d-a844-b5ab7fa63602) and posts a notice on the Sky Forum.
