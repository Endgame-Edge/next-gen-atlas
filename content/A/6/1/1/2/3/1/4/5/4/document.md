###### A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.5.4 - Updating Of Status [Core]

Upon offboarding, the Delegate’s status in [A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.8 - Registry Of Delegates](ce2e0c1b-be0f-468d-a844-b5ab7fa63602) is updated to Inactive. GROVE delegators must manually revoke their delegations and redelegate if they wish to continue participating in Grove governance.
