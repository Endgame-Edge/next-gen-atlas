###### A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.3.4.2 - Abstaining For Non-Disclosed Conflicts [Core]

Abstaining for any reason other than a disclosed conflict is treated as non-performance under [A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.5 - Delegate Offboarding](f0642330-3841-4567-ab0d-c18349538dc9).
