###### A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.2 - How Delegation Works [Core]

GROVE holders may assign ("delegate") the full voting power of their wallet to an Active Delegate at any time (see [A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.8 - Registry of Delegates](ce2e0c1b-be0f-468d-a844-b5ab7fa63602)). The key features of delegation are specified in the subdocuments herein.
