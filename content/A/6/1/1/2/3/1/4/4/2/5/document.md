###### A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.4.2.5 - Grounds for Disqualification [Core]

Submission of fraudulent information, criminal indictment for financial crime, or repeated governance negligence (see [A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.5.2 - Non-Performance Removal](7155a5dd-efa5-40cd-b09b-654f2fc2a5c4)) constitutes grounds for the Grove Foundation to remove the Delegate under [A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.5.3 – Emergency Removal](724ed92e-3f0a-416e-b4c5-3476c03f00af).
