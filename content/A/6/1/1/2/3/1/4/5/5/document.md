###### A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.5.5 - Term-End Automatic Offboarding [Core]

At the end of a Delegate's six (6) month term (see [A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.4.3 - Minimum Term](98b84d0b-1efd-4cc6-8f43-9563a81f6b09)), if the Grove Foundation has not recorded a re-approval, the Delegate is automatically offboarded effective 00:00 UTC on the day after term end. The Operational Facilitator updates the Registry and posts a notice on the Sky Forum.
