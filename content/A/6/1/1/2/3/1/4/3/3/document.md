###### A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.3.3 - Vote on Every Proposal [Core]

The Delegate must cast a vote (For / Against) on 100% of governance proposals within the designated voting window (see [A.6.1.1.2.2.2.2.2.1.2.1.4 - Root Edit Token Holder Vote](f6dd56ae-ee72-4109-be99-eaf69c92c3be)).
