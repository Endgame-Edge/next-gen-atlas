###### A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.4.3 - Minimum Term [Core]

Effective upon approval of this proposal, Delegates are appointed by the Grove Foundation to fixed six (6) month terms aligned to calendar half-years (January 1–June 30; July 1–December 31). To continue beyond a term, a Delegate must be re-approved by the Grove Foundation prior to term end; absent re-approval, the Delegate is automatically offboarded at term end pursuant to [A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.5.5 - Term-End Automatic Offboarding](1ac3bff3-c369-49d0-888b-9a777a4f7247).
