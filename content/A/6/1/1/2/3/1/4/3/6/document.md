###### A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.3.6 - Maintain Independence [Core]

The Delegate must disclose conflicts of interest and abstain where impartiality is compromised (see [A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.3.4.1 - Disclosure Of Conflicts](8a63c442-e72f-4907-aef4-2879ff088814)). Failure to meet these obligations is grounds for offboarding (see [A.6.1.1.2.3.1.4.5 - Delegate Offboarding](f0642330-3841-4567-ab0d-c18349538dc9)).
