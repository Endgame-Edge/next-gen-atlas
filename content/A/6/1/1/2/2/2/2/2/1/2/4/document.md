###### A.6.1.1.2.2.2.2.2.1.2.4 - Short-Term Transitionary Measures [Core]

During the initial decentralization phase of GROVE token, all decisions on the parameters specified in [A.6.1.1.2.2.6.1 - Allocation System Primitive](fecdf649-666c-4196-a046-b2eaf76574d3) resulting in an increase of on-chain risk to the ecosystem will be subject to approval by the [A.1.8.1.1 - Core Council Risk Advisor](d80c8f64-b3f6-430d-bf62-8e50a3783e73).
The approval must be posted on the Forum thread of the proposal by Thursday, 16:00 UTC of week 1 of the Prime Spell process, following the timelines established in the [A.1.10.2.3.2.2.3 - Prime Spell Process Breakdown](a67d672a-83f4-46db-adfc-f08004d3f152). A proposal cannot move to Sky Executive Vote without the Core Council Risk Advisor's approval, even if it had successfully passed the vote by GROVE token holders.

This transitionary phase will last until Sky determines that the GROVE token is decentralized enough to allow for meaningful governance by token holders. At such time, control will fully transition to Grove Governance.
